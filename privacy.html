Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026 · Applies to the Easy Story Downloader Android app and this website.

Easy Story Downloader helps you save Instagram stories, reels, posts and highlights that you have permission to download. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Instagram or Meta.

1. Data stored on your device

  • Instagram session cookies — saved locally so the in-app browser stays logged in and can request media on your behalf. They are never sent to our servers.
  • Download history — media URL, thumbnail URL, username, caption, local file path, file size and timestamp, in a local SQLite database.
  • Settings — theme choice and preferences.

Uninstalling the app removes this local data.

2. Data stored in Firebase

The app uses Google Firebase Firestore to sync your download history:

  • a randomly generated installation ID (not your name, email or advertising ID);
  • download metadata: media URL, thumbnail URL, username, caption, local path, size and timestamp.

Media files are never uploaded. Only metadata is synced. Firestore is hosted in the nam5 (United States) multi-region location.

3. Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob. When ads are enabled, Google may collect device identifiers (including the advertising ID), approximate location derived from IP, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. See Google’s advertising policy. You can opt out of personalised advertising in your device’s Google settings. Ad frequency and placement are controlled remotely by the app’s configuration.

4. Third parties

  • Google Firebase (Firestore) — data processing.
  • Google AdMob — advertising.
  • Instagram/Meta — the app connects to Instagram at your request using your session.

We do not sell your personal data.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • Local history: delete individual items or use Settings → Clear library.
  • Firestore history: deleting an item in the app deletes the matching Firestore record.
  • To delete the whole installation record, email us with your installation ID.
  • Uninstalling the app stops future sync.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Security

Network traffic uses HTTPS. Session cookies are stored in the app’s private sandbox. No method of storage is 100% secure.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change and the new version will be posted on this page.

9. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: privacy@easystorydownloader.com.
