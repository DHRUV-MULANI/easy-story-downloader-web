FAQ

Does this website download media?

No. This site is the app’s home and privacy centre. The downloader runs in the Android app; a web downloader would require a server backend and is not offered here.

Do I need an Instagram account?

Public reels and posts can be resolved without logging in. Stories and highlights require an Instagram login because Instagram only serves that media to a logged-in session.

Is this affiliated with Instagram?

No. Easy Story Downloader is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Instagram or Meta.

What data does the app store?

Session cookies stay on your device. Download metadata can sync to Firebase. Media files are never uploaded. See the privacy policy.

Can I change the theme without an update?

Yes. Colours, copy, announcements, parser order and ads are remote configuration. New features and fixes still require an app update.
